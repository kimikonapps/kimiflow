## 🟡 Phase 4 — Plan-gate (binary; three global rounds maximum)

- **Prose quality (same model pass):** Reuse the run-loaded `workflow-prose-quality.md` contract and apply it before output. Read it now only if this is the first decision-bearing artifact or a fresh-context/digest change invalidated the loaded copy; add no agent, call, step, or gate.

The review contract below is complete for the normal path. Do not reread the large `reference.md` review rubric.

0. **Mechanical preflight:** after the Phase-4 `phase-read`, run `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT:-$CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/hooks/plan-blocker-gate.sh .kimiflow/<slug>` once (Codex: `KIMIFLOW_HOST=codex` and `KIMIFLOW_PLUGIN_ROOT`). `OPEN` is required before reviewers. Do not probe this gate in Phase 3. New Convergence-Contract runs carry `Plan review contract: 1` plus `Plan review profile: standard|contract`; the profile selects reviewer topology, not another planning artifact. Audit keeps its existing profile. Confirm every AC maps to a task/test and no orphan task exists.
1. **Freeze once, review broadly, then edit once.** Hash the exact current `PLAN.md` (audit: `AUDIT.md` plus `AUDIT-INTENT.md`) and do not mutate the basis until the round's complete candidate batch exists. Round 1 dispatches every scheduled lens independently on those same bytes. `small` schedules exact lens IDs `a,b`; `large`, audit, and profile=`contract` schedule exact IDs `a,b,c`; the gates reject narrower or reordered lists. On Codex each round-1 reviewer uses `fork_turns="none"` and receives one self-contained packet with the literal workspace/plugin/run paths, exact basis digest, exact artifact paths, its lens/coverage assignment, evidence threshold, and output path/grammar; it does not inherit the orchestration transcript or rediscover helpers. Because the parent plus planning worker occupy two of Codex's four seats, dispatch A+B together, wait once with `timeout_ms=3600000`, then dispatch C after a seat returns; never attempt a known-over-capacity third child, call `list_agents` to recover from it, or poll every 30 seconds. Other hosts may fill their actually available seats. If independent seats are unavailable, run the same isolated packets sequentially on the frozen basis and record the fallback; deliberately selected `balanced`/`cheap` seats never issue the verdict. One round-1 lens is cross-family by default when available (prefer C for contract profile, otherwise B). Pi Main uses visible `stage=confirmed` Scouts when its crew is active; Captain and the implementation Ship never self-review.
   - **A — intent/completeness/subtraction:** trace every requirement to measurable AC, task, artifact, and check; find contradictions or invented scope; remove unsupported work.
   - **B — failure/security/evidence:** concrete error, security, data-loss, identity/replay, path/evidence, rollback, and architecture-falsifier risks; demand architecture change only with an exact failing scenario/executable check or named-invariant violation. Audit also refutes every deletion by hunting live callers.
   - **C — state/contract/aggregation:** directly enumerate material legal, illegal, missing, and boundary states from intent/acceptance; falsify lifecycle and time order, cross-row identity, derived formulas, denominators, thresholds, and verdict precedence. Report uncovered combinations without asking the planner to prebuild a cross-product matrix.
   - A BLOCKER/HIGH needs a cited intent/AC, required constraint, current compatibility rule, project standard, or demonstrable security/data-loss failure. Optional robustness, taste, future scope, and hypothetical unsupported requirements do not block. Each reviewer enumerates **all** independently evidenced material candidates; never stop after the first.
2. **Candidate and coverage contract (`Plan review contract: 1`).** Each `.kimiflow/<slug>/plan-review-candidates/r<N>-<lens>.md` starts with `BASIS plan_sha256=<64hex>`, contains at least one `COVERAGE <domain> :: status=checked|not_applicable :: evidence=<ref>` line, then either `NONE` or every candidate as the exact single-line grammar `CANDIDATE <SEVERITY> <ref> :: <claim> :: family=<stable-family> :: verify=command:<method>` or `CANDIDATE <SEVERITY> <ref> :: <claim> :: family=<stable-family> :: verify=verifier:<method>`. The literal `verify=` is mandatory; do not write a bare trailing `verifier:` or `command:` field. Across the scheduled lenses the exact domains are `intent-trace,state-space,identity-binding,evidence-safety,time-lifecycle,aggregation-verdict,scope-subtraction`. A bare `NONE` is invalid. Reviewers do not read `reference.md`; inline the relevant lens, evidence threshold, grammar, assigned coverage domains, and prose-quality path in <18 lines.
3. **Seal the round in one command.** Candidate lines already carry their verification method and may be BLOCKER/HIGH only when independently evidenced; uncertain or advisory concerns stay MEDIUM/LOW. After every scheduled candidate file exists, do not manually create findings, evidence receipts, hashes, saturation JSON, recovery markers, or Active-Run pins. Run exactly `plan-review-gate.sh seal --run .kimiflow/<slug> --round <N> --expect <lensCSV> --write`. The helper deterministically promotes material candidates, groups their declared root families, writes and digest-binds all administrative artifacts, pins the immutable receipt, validates the full history, and invokes the binary resolver. A fresh round that omits a prior family supplies its digest-bound negative resolution automatically. `CLOSED/open-findings` means repair every emitted family together, rerun the Phase-4 mechanical preflight once on the repaired plan, then dispatch the next frozen round. `OPEN/clean` advances. A malformed candidate or incomplete coverage is repaired before sealing; do not spend another semantic review call on transport.
4. **Exactly three global round roles:** round 1 = exhaustive independent A/B(/C) discovery; batch-repair all material families. Round 2 = one fresh independent closeout verifier checks every repaired family, its counterexamples, and the whole contract, then writes the exact A/B(/C) coverage partitions before one seal; do not respawn the three discovery seats. Batch any remaining material families. Round 3, only if round 2 remains material, uses one new fresh resolution verifier for the same complete partitions; the seal rejects every new BLOCKER/HIGH. These later files are coverage partitions from one independent verifier, not claims of three independent opinions. Rounds remain global, monotonically increasing, and immutable across every PLAN/model/strategy change. `C=3` is absolute; no recovery epoch, rewording, model switch, clean file, or new PLAN hash can start round 4.
5. **Routing:** `incomplete|malformed` repairs transport in the same round. `open-findings|root-class-repeated|oscillation|reappeared|cap-reached` changes the technical strategy within the remaining global rounds. A material defect at round 3 or any `review-limit-reached` preserves the blocker and routes to AC-preserving decomposition or an explicit follow-up decision—never an automatic fourth round and never a silent downgrade. User input remains limited to a genuinely material authority/scope/risk decision. Flow-5 audits retain their direct immutable findings ledger and explicit `resolve-review-gate.sh` call because they have no build plan to seal.
6. The seal helper records the compact `REVIEW.md` round summary. On `OPEN/clean`, continue. `--prepare` parks with phases 0–4 done and prints `/kimiflow --resume <slug>`.
7. **Pre-build decision boundary.** Use the bounded "Build Preview / Risk Gate" section already supplied in the phase packet. Show **Will build/fix · Not included · Important decisions · Risks/irreversibility · Effort**. For schema 4+, record `Build risk: none|required` and run `resolve-build-gate.sh decide --state .kimiflow/<slug>/STATE.md`; risk `none` continues, while a material typed risk pauses once. Schema-3 runs retain their legacy preview behavior.
